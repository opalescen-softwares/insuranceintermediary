Who We Are

Opalescent Insurance Agency is a trusted insurance broker dedicated to providing comprehensive, affordable and reliable insurance solutions to individuals, families and businesses across Kenya.

Our experienced advisors work closely with clients to understand their needs and recommend insurance products that provide real peace of mind.
